Project overview 01 02
01
A considered place to call home

Urbanrise The World of Joy is a residential apartment project in Siruseri, OMR, Chennai, spread across 10.13 acres. The project offers 3 BHK apartments ranging from 1,033 to 1,086 sq.ft. and is currently nearing completion.

02
Amenities & connectivity

The project features a 45,000 sq.ft. clubhouse along with rooftop amenities, landscaped spaces, and fitness and recreational facilities. Located close to the OMR IT corridor, it is approximately 10 minutes from Sholinganallur, offering convenient access to key employment and lifestyle destinations.
