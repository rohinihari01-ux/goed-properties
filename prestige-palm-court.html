About Prestige Palm Court

Project overview KKR Nagar · Madhavaram
A large residential community for modern families

Prestige Palm Court is a residential apartment project located in KKR Nagar, Madhavaram, Chennai. Spread across 8 acres, the project offers thoughtfully planned 1, 2 and 3 BHK apartments designed to suit the needs of modern homebuyers.

With 900+ residential units and apartment sizes ranging from 641 Sq.Ft. to 1,887 Sq.Ft., Prestige Palm Court offers a range of home configurations for different family requirements and budgets. The development rises across 22 floors, creating a large residential community in Madhavaram.

The project offers homes priced from approximately ₹67.5 Lakh to ₹1.99 Crore, with possession scheduled for December 2029.
