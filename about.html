Leadership & Vision

Mr. Srinivasan C

Founder, Goed Properties & Promoters

Goed Properties & Promoters is a trusted Chennai-based real estate company founded by Mr. Srinivasan C, a visionary entrepreneur with over 15 years of experience in the real estate industry. His passion for connecting people with the right property and his commitment to delivering value have made Goed Properties a preferred choice for homebuyers and investors. Mr. Srinivasan C focuses on helping clients build long-term wealth through smart real estate investments.

Supported by a dedicated team of 16 experienced professionals, we specialize in buying, selling, and investing in residential plots, villas, apartments, farmlands, commercial properties, and bank e-auction properties. Every property is carefully selected and legally verified to ensure a safe and rewarding investment.

Whether you're a first-time homebuyer, investor, NRI, or developer, we provide end-to-end assistance—from property selection and legal verification to documentation and registration—making your real estate journey simple, secure, and hassle-free.
